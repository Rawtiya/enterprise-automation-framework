# Enterprise Automation & Process Excellence Framework

A practical enterprise framework for identifying, redesigning, prioritizing, automating, governing, and scaling business processes.

## Purpose

The framework combines Process Excellence, Lean Six Sigma, automation, AI, workflow, and data-driven decision making to create measurable business outcomes.

## PEAK Framework

**P — Process**

Discover and understand the current process, customer needs, pain points, waste, and performance.

**E — Evaluate**

Assess process maturity, automation feasibility, business value, risk, complexity, and technology options.

**A — Automate**

Redesign the process and implement the appropriate combination of workflow, RPA, APIs, AI, analytics, or other digital technologies.

**K — Keep Improving**

Govern the solution, measure performance, validate benefits, manage exceptions, and continuously improve.

Core Principles
Do not automate waste.
Simplify before automating.
Standardize before scaling.
Select technology based on the process problem.
Design for measurable business outcomes.
Build controls into the automation.
Measure realized benefits, not just automation activity.
Treat automation as an enterprise capability, not a collection of projects.
