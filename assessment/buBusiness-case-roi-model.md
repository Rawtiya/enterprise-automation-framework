# Business Case & ROI Model

## Purpose

The Business Case & ROI Model evaluates whether an automation opportunity creates sufficient measurable value to justify investment.

The model considers:

- Implementation investment
- Operating cost
- Capacity release
- Cost reduction
- Quality improvement
- Risk reduction
- Customer impact
- Payback
- ROI

# 3. Capacity Release

Automation may release employee capacity without necessarily reducing headcount.

### Formula

`Capacity Released = Current Annual Manual Hours - Future Annual Manual Hours`

Capacity should be classified as:

- Redeployed
- Avoided future hiring
- Overtime reduction
- Available capacity
- Actual cost reduction

Avoid treating released capacity as financial savings unless the financial impact is validated.

---

# 4. ROI

### Annual Net Benefit

`Annual Net Benefit = Annual Benefit - Annual Operating Cost`

### ROI

`ROI = (Annual Net Benefit - Annualized Investment) / Investment × 100`

The organization may use its approved finance methodology for official ROI reporting.

---

# 5. Payback Period

### Formula

`Payback Period = Initial Investment / Annual Net Benefit`

The result should be expressed in years or months.

# 7. Benefits Classification

Every claimed benefit should be classified.

| Benefit Type | Definition |
|---|---|
| Hard Savings | Direct measurable reduction in cost |
| Soft Savings | Quantifiable benefit without immediate P&L reduction |
| Capacity Release | Employee capacity made available |
| Cost Avoidance | Future cost that is avoided |
| Risk Avoidance | Potential loss or exposure reduced |
| Revenue Enablement | Capability that supports additional revenue |

---

# 8. Benefits Realization

The business case is not considered complete at deployment.

Benefits should be tracked through:

**Baseline → Target → Actual → Validated**

Example:

| KPI | Baseline | Target | Actual | Validated |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Manual Hours | 10,000 | 5,000 | 4,100 | Yes |
| Error Rate | 5.2% | <2% | 1.4% | Yes |
| Cycle Time | 48 hrs | 24 hrs | 19 hrs | Yes |
| Annual Benefit | ₹24L | ₹20L+ | ₹21L | Pending |

---

# 9. Decision Principle

A financially attractive automation opportunity should still be assessed against:

- Strategic alignment
- Customer impact
- Risk
- Compliance
- Technology sustainability
- Change impact
- Process maturity

> **ROI supports the decision; it does not replace Process Excellence judgment.**
