# Automation Scoring Rubric

## Purpose

The scoring rubric establishes consistent definitions for evaluating automation opportunities.

Each assessment dimension is scored from **1 to 5**.

The score should be supported by available process data wherever possible.

---

| Score | Definition |
|---|---|

| 2 | Low effort or cost impact |
| 3 | Moderate effort or cost impact |

| 5 | Very significant effort or cost impact |

---

# 3. Scoring Discipline

Assessors should:

1. Use evidence wherever available.
2. Avoid scoring based solely on perception.
3. Document assumptions.
4. Identify data gaps.
5. Validate material scores with the Process Owner.
6. Reassess scores when process conditions change.

---

# 4. Governance Rule

Scores should be reviewed by the appropriate Process Excellence or Automation governance forum before major investment decisions.

The scoring model supports decision-making; it does not replace management judgment.
