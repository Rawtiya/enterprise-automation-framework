# Benefits Realization Framework

## Purpose

The Benefits Realization Framework ensures that automation delivers the business outcomes identified during the business case.

Deployment is not considered the end of the automation initiative.

The initiative is successful only when expected benefits are measured, validated, and sustained.

5. Benefit Validation

Benefits should progress through four states:

Planned

Benefit identified in the business case.

Realized

Performance improvement observed after implementation.

Validated

Benefit confirmed by the appropriate business or finance owner.

Sustained

Benefit continues to be achieved over an agreed period.

6. Capacity Release vs Cost Savings

Capacity released should not automatically be classified as financial savings.

For example:

10,000 hours manual effort
        ↓
4,000 hours after automation
        ↓
6,000 hours capacity released

The 6,000 hours may be:

Redeployed to higher-value work
Used to absorb business growth
Used to avoid future hiring
Converted into actual cost reduction

Only validated financial impact should be reported as hard savings.

8. Benefits Attainment
Formula
Benefits Attainment =
Actual Validated Benefit / Planned Benefit × 100

Example:

Planned Benefit = ₹50 lakh
Validated Benefit = ₹42 lakh

Benefits Attainment = 84%
9. Post-Implementation Review

A post-implementation review should evaluate:

Was the expected benefit achieved?
Which assumptions were correct?
Which assumptions were incorrect?
Did process performance improve?
Did automation create new risks?
Did exception patterns change?
Is additional optimization required?
Can the solution be scaled?
10. Continuous Improvement

Benefits realization should feed back into Process Excellence.

Measure
   ↓
Analyze
   ↓
Improve
   ↓
Re-measure
   ↓
Standardize
   ↓
Scale

Automation should therefore become part of the organization's continuous improvement cycle.

11. Executive Principle

Automation is not successful because it went live. It is successful when the intended business outcome is achieved and sustained.
