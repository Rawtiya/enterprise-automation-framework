# Master Automation Lifecycle

## Enterprise Automation & Process Excellence Framework

The Enterprise Automation Framework follows seven stages:

> **Discover → Diagnose → Design → Decide → Automate → Govern → Scale**

The lifecycle is designed to ensure that automation follows Process Excellence principles and delivers measurable, sustainable business value.

---

## 1. Discover

### Objective

Identify and define business processes that may have improvement or automation potential.

### Key Activities

- Process inventory
- Stakeholder identification
- Customer pain-point identification
- Volume and effort assessment
- Initial opportunity identification
- Process owner identification

### Key Questions

- What problem are we trying to solve?
- Who is affected?
- What is the current process?
- What is the business impact?
- Why does the problem matter?

### Outputs

- Opportunity statement
- Process owner
- Initial process profile
- Initial business problem

### Gate

**Is there a meaningful business problem or opportunity?**

---

# 2. Diagnose

### Objective

Understand the current process and identify root causes.

### Key Activities

- SIPOC
- Process mapping
- Value Stream Mapping
- Waste analysis
- Root-cause analysis
- Performance measurement
- Variation analysis
- Data analysis

### Key Questions

- Where does waste occur?
- What causes delays?
- What causes defects?
- Where are the bottlenecks?
- What creates variation?
- Is the technology actually the problem?

### Outputs

- Current-state process
- Performance baseline
- Root-cause analysis
- Waste assessment
- Improvement opportunities

### Gate

**Do we understand the problem and its root causes?**

---

# 3. Design

### Objective

Create a better future-state process before selecting automation technology.

### Key Activities

- Process simplification
- Elimination of non-value-added activities
- Standardization
- Control redesign
- To-Be process design
- Technology-independent solution design

### Key Principle

> **Do not automate the As-Is process. Design the right To-Be process first.**

### Outputs

- To-Be process
- Future-state controls
- Standardized process
- Business rules
- Performance targets

### Gate

**Is the future-state process simpler, standardized, measurable, and fit for purpose?**

Enterprise Feedback Loop

The lifecycle is not linear.

Performance data from Govern and Scale feeds back into Discover and Diagnose.

              ┌──────────────────────────────┐
              │                              │
              ↓                              │
Discover → Diagnose → Design → Decide → Automate
                                      ↓
                                   Govern
                                      ↓
                                    Scale
                                      │
                                      └──────→ Discover

This creates a continuous Process Excellence cycle.

Executive Operating Principle

Discover the right problem. Diagnose the root cause. Design the right process. Decide the right intervention. Automate where it creates value. Govern what you deploy. Scale what works.

The framework therefore treats automation as an enterprise capability within Process Excellence, rather than automation as an objective in itself.
